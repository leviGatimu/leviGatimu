## `01` — A little about me

I'm **Levi**, a student developer and builder from **Rwanda**.

Most of what I work on sits somewhere between **software engineering, hardware, design, and automation**. I enjoy taking an idea from a rough concept all the way to something people can actually use.

That usually means doing more than writing code:

**problem → research → design → architecture → build → test → improve**

I'm particularly interested in:

* Full-stack application development
* Product & UI/UX design
* Robotics and embedded systems
* AI and automation
* Drones and aviation
* Building technology around real-world problems

I'm still learning, and that's kind of the point.

## `03` — Selected projects

These are some of the projects that represent the kind of problems I like working on.

### `New Generation Academic System`

A school platform designed around the day-to-day needs of **students, teachers and administrators**.

Rather than being just a website, the goal is to create a connected system for things such as academic information, timetables, assignments, announcements and communication.

**Built around:**
`Web Applications` · `Authentication` · `Role-based Systems` · `Databases` · `UX`

---

### `Trace`

A desktop productivity and activity system focused on understanding how time is actually being spent on a computer.

The project explores background tracking, application activity, history and a dashboard for turning raw activity into something useful.

**Built around:**
`Electron` · `Desktop Systems` · `Windows APIs` · `Databases` · `Data Visualization`

---

### `Tembera Rwanda`

A tourism-focused platform designed to make exploring Rwanda easier by connecting visitors with places, services and useful information across the country.

The project is particularly focused on **navigation, discovery and local context**, rather than simply presenting another tourism website.

**Built around:**
`Maps` · `Location Services` · `UX` · `Full-Stack Development`

---

### `Sentinel Signals`

An AI-assisted traffic management concept exploring how intelligent systems could improve the way traffic signals respond to real-world conditions.

**Built around:**
`AI Concepts` · `Automation` · `Traffic Systems` · `Embedded Technology`

---

### `Automated Trash Bin Robot`

A robotics project exploring autonomous movement and automated waste collection.

The goal was simple: take a normal everyday object and make it capable of responding to its environment.

**Built around:**
`Arduino` · `Ultrasonic Sensors` · `Motors` · `Automation`

---

### `AI Personal Trainer`

A physical computing concept combining sensors with software logic to provide basic exercise and heart-rate guidance.

**Built around:**
`Arduino` · `Sensors` · `Embedded Systems` · `Automation`
