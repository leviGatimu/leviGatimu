## `01` — About Me

I'm **Levi Gatimu**, a developer and builder focused on the intersection of **software, hardware, robotics, AI, and aviation technology**.

I enjoy taking an idea from a rough concept and turning it into something people can actually **use, interact with, and understand**.

My work ranges from web platforms and desktop applications to Arduino systems, autonomous robots, mapping applications, and experimental AI projects.

I'm especially interested in building technology that has a **real-world purpose** rather than simply building things for the sake of building them.
